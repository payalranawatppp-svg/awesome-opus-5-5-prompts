## Main caption

Your LinkedIn doesn't need more hours. It needs a system. 👇

Most business owners don't have a "content problem". They have a time problem, so the page goes quiet and the opportunities go with it.

Here's the simple routine that keeps a LinkedIn page alive without taking over your week:

1️⃣ One useful post a week
Share something your clients actually ask you about: a lesson, a mistake you see often, or a quick how-to. Useful beats polished.

2️⃣ 10 minutes of genuine replies a day
Comment on posts from people in your industry. Add a thought or a question, not just "Great post!" Replies build visibility faster than posting more.

3️⃣ Personal outreach, not templates
When you connect or message, mention something specific about them. One thoughtful message beats ten copy-pastes.

4️⃣ Check your inbox daily
Warm conversations go cold fast. A quick reply is often the difference between a chat and a lead.

5️⃣ Batch it
Block one hour a week to draft your post and line up who to engage with. Consistency comes from a routine, not motivation.

Start small: one useful post and a few genuine replies this week. 🎯
