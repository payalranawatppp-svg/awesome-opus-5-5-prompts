## Flow tips

- Make the **front office background image** first, then the cast still with both women in it. Generate every clip
  from the two-shot still, or from the last frame of the previous clip, so faces, hair, clothes and the room stay the
  same.
- Flow renders on-screen text badly, so keep the monitor angled away. The real dashboard goes in during the edit.
- If both women talk at once, put the speaker first: "Only Tanya speaks…" or "Only Amanda speaks…".
- To keep it looking filmed, not generated: if a take looks waxy or too smooth, add "documentary realism, natural skin, steady tripod shot, subtle film grain". If movement looks floaty, add "natural, unhurried human movement, real physics".
- If a voice drifts between clips, send the clips anyway. I'll match each voice in the edit, re-voicing whole lines,
  never spliced words.
