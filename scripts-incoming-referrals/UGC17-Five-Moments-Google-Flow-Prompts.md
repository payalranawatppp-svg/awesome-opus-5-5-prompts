### E1 · Hook: Ethan on camera (7–12 s)

### Fix 1 · Someone leaves, and the referral history leaves with them (12–20 s) — dashboard

Referral timeline and history: entries pop in with a name and a time ("Received · Rachel Moore · Nov 5, 9:12 AM").

> **Ethan (VO, not in a prompt):** When someone leaves, the referral shouldn't leave with them. Every update and message stays on the case, so it lives in the platform, not one person's head.

Chips: ✕ "Jenna knew" → ✓ Full history on the case

### S2 · Case 2: "Can you just text me that X-ray?" (≈20–27 s)

- **Dr. Ruiz:** Can you just text me Olivia's file? I'm running between rooms.
- **Rachel:** From my phone?
- **Dr. Ruiz:** Yeah, that's a bad idea, isn't it.

### Fix 2 · "Can you just text me that X-ray?" (20–35 s) — dashboard

Referral detail page; the cursor opens the X-ray and attachments.

> **Ethan (VO, not in a prompt):** Texting X-rays from a personal phone is one of the most common HIPAA slip-ups. Keep it on the case, and open it from any browser.

Chips: ✕ Personal phone → ✓ On the case, secure

### S3 · Case 3: the hallway question nobody remembers (≈35–42 s)

- **Rachel:** Doc, Olivia's pano is from 2023. Do we take a new one before her consult?
- **Dr. Ruiz:** Good question. Remind me after lunch.

### Fix 3 · The hallway question nobody remembers (35–50 s) — dashboard

Administrative Notes: Rachel's @mention about the 2023 pano, the "1 unread @mention" pill, Dr. Ruiz's quoted reply, Kevin confirms. Label "Private · only your office".

> **Ethan (VO, not in a prompt):** Hallway questions disappear. Put it in a private note and tag the doctor. The answer waits on the case, and the other office never sees it.

Chips: ✕ "Remind me after lunch" → ✓ Tagged, answered, private

### S4 · Case 4: the consult letter retyped at 6:40 p.m. (≈50–57 s)

- **Kevin:** You're still here?
- **Rachel:** Retyping Dr. Ruiz's consult letter. Same letter, new patient name, every time.

### Fix 4 · The consult letter retyped at 6:40 p.m. (50–65 s) — dashboard

Prepare Consultation Note: the letter fills in from the template, Show values ↔ Show codes, Sign and Send, signature draws, "All done", success toast.

> **Ethan (VO, not in a prompt):** Turn that Word letter into a template once. Patient and doctor details fill themselves in, the doctor signs digitally, and it goes straight back.

Chips: ✕ Retyped every time → ✓ Template, signed, sent

### S5 · Case 5: the patient declined, nobody told the referring dentist (≈65–72 s)

- **Dr. Whitaker:** Daniel Ortiz is back for his cleaning. Did he ever have that implant done?
- **Megan:** I… don't know. Nobody ever told us.

### Fix 5 · The patient declined, and nobody told the referring dentist (65–80 s) — dashboard

Status picker on Daniel Ortiz's case → Declined Appointment, a short note posts to Office Communication, and the referring office sees it on their side.

> **Ethan (VO, not in a prompt):** If a patient declines, mark it on the case with a short note. The referring dentist sees it the same day, not at the next recall.

Chips: ✕ Found out at recall → ✓ Same-day update

### E7a · Close, part 1: Ethan on camera (80–84 s)
