# Script 20: "Did They Ever Send It?" · Google Flow prompts

12 clips × 8 s ≈ 96 s · horizontal 16:9 · three characters: Greg (Gentle Dental, general dentist), Tanya and Amanda (St. Andrew's Family Dental, specialist) · professional live-action look.

Problem: the referral loop that never closes. Sarah is about to get her crown at Gentle Dental, and the specialist's signed completion note never arrived. Greg calls Tanya at St. Andrew's; the note was signed Friday but nobody is sure how it went out. Nobody is blamed: every office sends things a different way. Amanda introduces Tooth Referral to Tanya, they invite Greg's office, and one month later it all lives on the case.

## The script

### Act 1 · the problem (Greg calls Tanya)

**01 · Ten minutes** (Gentle Dental)  
Greg: "Sarah's crown is in ten minutes... and her completion note never came."  

**02 · The other side** (St. Andrew's)  
Tanya: "St. Andrew's Family Dental, this is Tanya."  

**03 · Emailed twice** (Gentle Dental)  
Greg: "Hi Tanya, it's Greg at Gentle Dental. Did Sarah Collins' completion note go out? I emailed twice."  

**04 · Email or fax?** (St. Andrew's)  
Tanya: "One second, Greg."  
Tanya: "Did Sarah Collins' note go out by email or fax?"  
Amanda: "Fax, I think. Check the tray."  

**05 · Found it** (St. Andrew's)  
Tanya: "Found it. Greg, I'm so sorry, I'm faxing it right now."  

**06 · The patient waits** (Gentle Dental)  
Greg: "Thanks, Tanya."  
Greg: "Every office sends things a different way... and the patient waits."  

### Amanda and Tanya introduce Tooth Referral

**07 · Let's set up Tooth Referral** (St. Andrew's)  
Amanda: "This keeps happening. Let's set up Tooth Referral."  
Tanya: "Tooth Referral?"  
Amanda: "One case for every referral. Both offices on it."  

**08 · How it works** (St. Andrew's)  
Amanda: "Messages, X-rays, insurance, the signed note. All on the case. Gentle Dental sees it too."  
Tanya: "Let's invite Greg's office today."  

### Act 2 · one month later, with Tooth Referral

**09 · Already on the case** (Gentle Dental)  
Greg: "David's signed note is already on the case. Copying it into our electronic health record... done."  

**10 · Quiet phone** (St. Andrew's)  
Tanya: "Gentle Dental hasn't called once this week."  
Amanda: "They don't need to. They see the status, and they message us right on the case."  

**11 · Same place** (St. Andrew's)  
Tanya: "And when we need an X-ray or insurance from them?"  
Amanda: "Same place. Insurance is one secure link. Setup took five minutes."  

**12 · One conversation** (St. Andrew's)  
Tanya: "Feels like we're one office."  
Amanda: "Two offices. One conversation. That's Tooth Referral."
