## 3. Template for every piece, from now on

1. **Hook + problem as human error.** Busy days, someone out, a voicemail nobody followed up on, a coordinator
   leaves and nobody knows what's next. Never blame the staff.
2. **Form vs. ToothReferral.** A form sends an email. ToothReferral starts a shared, tracked case.
3. **Show the work.** Messages, notes from templates, signing or routing to the doctor, case presentations.
4. **ROI with numbers.** Monthly cost vs. potential return, with the math shown.
5. **Networking.** Offices that keep talking keep referring.
6. **CTA.** Free trial.

Throughout: be front-desk friendly, show the owner what they get, and follow the review rules (no "GP", no red, no
doctors' names, staff are Barbara, Tanya, Amanda and Greg, Gentle Dental / St. Andrew's Family Dental).
