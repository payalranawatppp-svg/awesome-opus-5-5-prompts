## The script

**01 · Since lunch**  
Amanda: "You've been staring at that screen since lunch. What's going on?"  

**02 · None of them match**  
Tanya: "I'm trying to figure out where one referral even stands. There's an email, a voicemail, a fax... and none of them match."  

**03 · She's right**  
Tanya: "And a patient just called, upset. She said nobody ever got back to her. Honestly? She's right."  

**04 · Losing steam**  
Amanda: "Hey, it's not you. Half the team's been out, it's the holidays..."  
Tanya: "...and we're losing steam."  

**05 · The old way**  
Tanya: "Every referral lives in someone's inbox or someone's head. If they're out, it's just... gone."  
Amanda: "Yeah. That's the old way."  

**06 · At my last office**  
Amanda: "At my last office, we replaced the form on our website with Tooth Referral. Every referral came in as one case."  
Tanya: "One case?"  

**07 · All in one place**  
Amanda: "It puts all the communication about that referral in one place. X-rays, insurance, messages, status. Whoever opens it sees everything."  

**08 · If I'm out tomorrow**  
Tanya: "So if I'm out tomorrow..."  
Amanda: "Greg opens it and knows exactly where it stands. No digging through anyone's email."  

**09 · They call constantly**  
Tanya: "And the dentist's office? They call us constantly."  
Amanda: "They see the status themselves. And they can message us right on the case."  

**10 · It just ran**  
Amanda: "That's what I liked. It didn't depend on who was working that day. The whole office just ran."  
Tanya: "That's what we need."  

**11 · Team meeting**  
Tanya: "Okay. I'm bringing this to the team meeting."  
Amanda: "Bring it. You might actually get to eat lunch."
