# Tooth Referral: product understanding (the brief for every future script)

One page that pulls together the client brief, Vejay's rules (11-04, 11-05, 11-11) and the platform audit (11-12).
Details live in SKILL.md, PLATFORM-FEATURES.md and DASHBOARD.md; when they disagree, the newest Vejay note wins.

## 1. What it is
Tooth Referral ("Just Refer it") is a **referral system for the whole dental team**, not a web form.
A web form collects details once and sends an email; then everything depends on someone remembering.
Tooth Referral turns every referral into **one case** that both offices work on until it's done:
patient, procedures, X-rays, insurance, a message thread between the two offices, status, signed notes, activity log.
It replaces the specialist's website form and the phone/fax/email/voicemail juggling around it.

## 2. Who it's for
- **Buyers:** dental specialists (endodontists, periodontists, oral surgeons, orthodontists) and their front office.
  The front desk and office manager influence most buying decisions: speak to them with respect.
- **Also on it:** the referring general dentist's office (sends, follows, gets the note back), assistants/RDHs,
  the dentist, the owner, and the patient (uploads insurance through a secure link).

## 3. The problem we tell (always human error, never staff fault)
Keeping up with emails, phone calls, voicemails and faxes; nobody sure where a referral stands; a patient waiting for
a call back; someone out sick or on holiday and the office losing steam; every referral living "in someone's inbox or
someone's head". Fix it with a system, not by blaming people. Don't exaggerate ("we lost 20 referrals" is out).

## 4. What the system does (the proof points)
| Benefit | Feature that proves it |
|---|---|
| Every referral in one place | Incoming list; one case per referral; All Attachments |
| Arrives complete | specialist's website referral form: procedures, tooth chart, up to 10 X-rays and documents, insurance via secure patient link |
| Direct communication | Office Communication: secure cloud messages on the case, @mentions, quoted replies |
| Nobody calls to ask | status chips + automatic status emails to the referring office; notifications feed |
| Anyone can pick it up | activity log (who did what, when); notes on the case |
| Notes without retyping | completion-note templates with smart variables (auto-fill patient, doctors, offices, X-rays, dates), Word/PDF import; copy notes into your own EHR; signed notes visible to both offices |
| Team-private talk | Administrative Notes (not shared with other offices) |
| Nothing stuck | Needs attention list; status board; Day-Prep Station for the doctor's day |
| The owner sees it all | analytics: funnel with drop-off, time to first contact, staff responsiveness, top senders, patient leakage and loop closure for referring offices; weekly/monthly reports |
| A growing network | Invite your Network; Connected Network Member; Access PIN; top senders / most responsive offices |
| Fast start | set up in about 5 minutes; HIPAA compliant and encrypted (confirm wording with Vejay for ads) |

## 5. The value per team member (the journey)
Referring office: sends once, sees status, gets the signed note back, no calling.
Front desk: one list, no hunting, the patient sends their own insurance.
Office manager: sees what's stuck and who's on it; work keeps moving when someone is out.
Assistant/RDH: X-rays already on the case; asks for a newer one in a message.
Dentist/specialist: notes from templates, signs, sends back; @mentioned only when needed; Day-Prep.
Owner: numbers on referrals, speed and lost patients; ROI.
**When every team member sees the value, everyone gets operational benefits.** That is the brand.

## 6. Message hierarchy (in order)
1. It's not just a form. It's a system for your team.
2. Web form vs system: say the difference plainly, one idea per slide.
3. Direct communication with the referring office (cloud messages) and the whole team on the referral.
4. Notes: build notes, templates, copy into the EHR, signed note back.
5. ROI (as "potential / up to", from SKILL.md 01) and network growth.
6. Set up in about 5 minutes.

## 7. Words
Use: referral system, one case, the whole team, direct communication, secure messages, on the case, the referring
office, general dentist, someone's inbox, keeps working after Submit, every conversation about a referral in one place.
Never: "GP", "shared inbox", doctors' names, "lost 20 referrals", per-incident dollar countdowns, mocking the front
desk, a Contact Us page/CTA (for now), unconfirmed features (Amended, Draft meaning, case-acceptance math).
