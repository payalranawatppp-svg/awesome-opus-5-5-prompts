Audience: oral surgeons, surgical assistants, practice managers. Ask: set up Tooth Referral (about 5 minutes).

**Caption**
Three tasks still take up hours in most oral surgery offices: emailing case updates to referring dentists, chasing referral statuses that nobody logged, and typing consultation notes by hand.

## Carousel B · Insurance cards, without the chase (endodontists)
Audience: endodontic front desks and office managers (also works for other specialists). Ask: set up Tooth Referral.

**Caption**
Endodontists, put down the microscope for a second. You'll still find the MB2.

How does your office get a referred patient's insurance card today? For many front desks it means photos on personal phones, faxes missing the back of the card, and patients rescheduled because coverage was not checked in time.

Tooth Referral replaces your referral form. On any referral, one click sends the patient a HIPAA-compliant secure link, protected by Cloudflare spam protection. The patient photographs the front and back of the card on their phone, with no account to create, and it lands on the case with the policy and group numbers. Out-of-network offices that file for their patients have the details before the first call.

Swipe to see each step.
