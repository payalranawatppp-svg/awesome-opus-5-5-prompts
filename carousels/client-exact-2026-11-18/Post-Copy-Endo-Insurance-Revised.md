# Endodontic insurance carousel: LinkedIn post copy (revised 24 Nov 2026; Vejay's original kept in ../client-revisions-2026-11-18/)

Endodontists put your front desk on automation without outsourcing!

Your patient already has the insurance card in their wallet. Let them send it to you in one secure step.

Every new referral starts the same way: call the patient, ask for the card, wait for a reply, then call again. Your front desk spends the day following up instead of scheduling.

ToothReferral replaces the static, one-way referral form with a referral case both offices share. One click on the case sends the patient a HIPAA-compliant secure link, protected by Cloudflare. The patient photographs the front and back of the card on their phone, no account needed, and both images arrive on the referral with the policy and group numbers.

Out of network and filing claims for your patients? The coverage details are on the case before anyone picks up the phone, so your team can talk through costs early and patients can say yes with confidence.

Insurance collected and on the case before the first call. Just refer it.
